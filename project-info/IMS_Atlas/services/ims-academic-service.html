Academic Service (`ims-academic-service`)

Manages the academic curriculum, years, terms, and subject definitions.

Context Map

graph LR Gateway --> Academic[Academic Service] Academic --> DB[(Academic DB)] Academic --> Notification[Notification Service]

Responsibilities

  • Curriculum Management: Defining courses, subjects, and topics.
  • Academic Calendar: Managing academic years and terms.
  • Offerings: Scheduling subject offerings for specific terms.
  • Announcements: Managing institutional announcements with audience filtering and expiry dates.
  • Messages & Broadcasts: Orchestrating multi-channel broadcasts (Email + In-App) and persisting broadcast history and user notifications.
