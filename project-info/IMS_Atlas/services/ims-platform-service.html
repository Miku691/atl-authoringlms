Platform Management Service

Handles platform-wide operations, leads management, and super-admin dashboards.

Responsibilities

  • Capture and manage "Book a Demo" lead requests.
  • Automate email notifications for lead captures and meeting scheduling.
  • Provide platform-wide analytics for Super Admins.
  • Manage global system settings and configurations.

Data Ownership

Entity Description
DemoRequest Stores lead information, preferred slots, and meeting links.

Communication Flows

  • Public: Captures demo requests via unprotected endpoints.
  • Secured: Restricts lead management to SUPER_ADMIN users.
  • Validation: Enforces Google Meet links for scheduling and mandatory feedback for cycle completion.
  • External: Integrates with ims-notification-service for verified email dispatch.
