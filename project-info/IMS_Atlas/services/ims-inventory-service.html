Inventory Service (`ims-inventory-service`)

Manages institutional inventory, asset registers, stock transactions, and suppliers.

Context & Unified Catalog Map

graph LR Gateway[API Gateway] --> Inventory[Inventory Service] Inventory --> DB[(Inventory DB)] Inventory -.-> Finance[Finance Service] subgraph Unified Catalog & Assets Cat[Inventory Category] --> Item[Inventory Item: CONSUMABLE / ASSET] Item -->|itemId| Asset[Asset Register Instance] end

Responsibilities & Architecture

  • Unified Master Catalog (`InventoryItem`): Centralized item catalog supporting both consumables (`CONSUMABLE`) and capital equipment (`ASSET`). Tracks aggregate stock quantities and reorder thresholds.
  • Asset Register (`Asset`): Maintaining official records of individual physical property instances, unique serial numbers, custodianship, locations, and status (`ACTIVE`, `UNDER_MAINTENANCE`, `DISPOSED`), explicitly linked back to master catalog items (`itemId`).
  • Stock Management: Tracking inventory stock levels, low-stock alerts, and departmental reorder requirements.
  • Suppliers: Managing vendor contacts, office addresses, and purchasing relationships.
  • Stock Transactions: Recording stock inflows (purchases) and outflows (usage) with audit trails, and triggering external purchase expense records in the Finance service.
